- Product Feedback
- Case Studies
- Onboarding Checklist
- Customer Outcomes
- Risk Mitigation

## ATS Formatting Checks

- Single-column layout.
- Standard section headings.
- No tables.
- No graphics, icons, columns, text boxes, or image-based content.
- Clear role title near the top.
- Skills section uses exact job-posting keywords.
- Experience bullets include customer success language and measurable product/workflow relevance.
- Contact details are plain text and easy for ATS tools to parse.

## Before Submitting

- Replace every bracketed placeholder, especially name, phone, email, LinkedIn, city, education, and certifications.
- Export to PDF after editing.
- Keep the file under 5 MB.
- Name the file clearly, for example: `FirstName_LastName_Customer_Success_Fellow_CV.pdf`.
- Do not include a photo unless the application specifically asks for one.

## Optional Improvements If You Have More Details

- Add any real customer support, sales, admin, teaching, volunteering, community leadership, or client communication experience.
- Add measurable results, such as number of users supported, pages managed, issues resolved, response time, or stakeholders coordinated.
- Add tools you have used, such as Google Workspace, Notion, Trello, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Slack, Excel, Airtable, ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
- Add a short certification section if you complete a free customer success, CRM, or AI productivity course before applying.
